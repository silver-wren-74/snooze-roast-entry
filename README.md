Entry for the [Snooze Button Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6)
competition on make.fun: one line roasting an alarm clock that rings at
6 AM on a Saturday morning.

`main.py` writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My alarm clock rang at 6 AM on a Saturday like a debt collector who
> found the weekend hiding under the bed and knocked anyway.
